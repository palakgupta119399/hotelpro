About HotelPro

Simple stays. Better journeys.

A hotel reservation website with room discovery, date-based bookings and reservation management.

🗺️

What you can do

Browse rooms, create an account, reserve a room for your travel dates, and view or cancel reservations from My Bookings. Administrators can add rooms, change room availability, and review booking and user lists.

Important: This app has a working local backend and database, but it is not yet deployed to a public URL and does not process payments or connect to an actual hotel's inventory.
